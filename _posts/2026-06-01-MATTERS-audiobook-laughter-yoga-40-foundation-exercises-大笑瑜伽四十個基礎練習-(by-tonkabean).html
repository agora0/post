本書簡介／Book Introduction


當笑聲遇上語言學習，會發生什麼事？

本書將40個大笑瑜伽練習，變成一本中英文對照的漫畫。每個練習包括一個小故事、中英文對話，以及句子練習。書中附有音檔，可以一邊聽一邊學。

你不需要擅長英文或廣東話，也不需要懂大笑瑜伽。只需要打開書，跟住聲音，笑著讀就好。

由東加豆製作，一個「笑住生活寫作課」的人。

What happens when laughter meets language learning?

This bilingual comic book turns 40 Laughter Yoga exercises into stories, Chinese-English dialogues, and sentence practices. It also comes with audio tracks so you can listen and learn at the same time.

You don’t need to be good at English or Cantonese, nor do you need to know Laughter Yoga. Just open the book, follow the audio, and read with a smile.

Created by Tonkabean—living and writing with a smile.
