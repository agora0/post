Laughter, Friendship & Spot the Difference (by tonkabean)


Hello everyone, I’m Tonkabean.

Today I’d like to share some exciting news with you: my latest fun picture book, Laughter, Friendship & Spot the Difference, is officially published!

I came up with the idea for this book about half a year ago. When I was a little girl, I loved playing a game called "Spot the Difference." I never expected that many years later, I would connect it with Laughter Yoga.

I am truly grateful to have found Laughter Yoga and joined this wonderful family. Here, I have experienced pure laughter and pure friendship, which has made my life so magical and precious.

This book is very simple. You can play to spot the differences, or you can just flip through the pages. You can enjoy it by yourself, or share it with a friend. The characters in this book are like friends you see every day. They are usually quiet, but in this book, they laugh, play, and get up to mischief.

If you’re feeling a bit stressed lately, or if you want a relaxing moment with your children or friends, open this book, find those hidden details, and enjoy a warm, heartfelt laugh together!

Finally, special thanks to Dr. Kataria, founder of the Laughter Yoga movement, for generously writing the foreword and for his warm support.

The book is now available. Feel free to check it out and share!
