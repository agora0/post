Challenge Game! Laughter Yoga Very Fun! 2026 In Different Mother Tongues.


Since joining Laughter Yoga, I've met friends from all over the world. 

Every time I see different faces and hear different accents on Zoom, I find it really interesting. 

Over time, I became curious about everyone's mother languages. So this activity came to mind — I wanted to hear everyone say a simple self-introduction in their own most familiar language. 

Just ten seconds, but each video reminded me that although our languages may be different, our laughter and connection are the same.
